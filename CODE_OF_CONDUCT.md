# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good faith, focus on technical evidence, accept constructive criticism, and respect differing experience levels.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Please include relevant context and links; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
